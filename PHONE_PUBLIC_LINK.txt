TEAMSYNC - PUBLIC HTTPS MEETING LINK

For a phone or computer outside your Wi-Fi, localhost cannot be used directly.
Use run_public.bat to create a temporary public HTTPS link.

1. Make sure TeamSync works locally at http://localhost:5173.
2. Double-click run_public.bat.
3. Wait for a URL such as https://random-name.trycloudflare.com.
4. The URL is also written to backend\public_url.txt.
5. In TeamSync, create a meeting and use Share Meeting -> WhatsApp.
6. The recipient can open the HTTPS meeting link from WhatsApp, Telegram, SMS, Gmail, or a browser.
7. Keep backend, frontend and the public-tunnel window running while the meeting is active.

Cloudflare Quick Tunnels do not require a Cloudflare account or domain, but they are temporary and intended for testing/demo use.

For a permanent production link, deploy TeamSync with a real HTTPS domain and configure the production URL.

Video calls can require a TURN server on restrictive mobile/corporate networks.
